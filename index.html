About Us

A Home Made for Summer

Welcome to 130 Island Beach Road — our family's beloved beach house on Drakes Island in Wells, Maine.

Experience classic Maine coastal living at this inviting beach house on idyllic Drakes Island located between Wells and Kennebunkport. A gorgeous ocean view and the sound of the waves set the tone for a relaxing getaway. Just steps from a private beach entrance, you’ll enjoy easy access to one of Wells’ most beautiful beaches—perfect for morning walks, sunbathing, and sunset views without the crowd.

With plenty of space to gather, the home comfortably sleeps 12+ guests and features 4 bathrooms, making it ideal for extended families or groups. Whether you’re enjoying the brand new upgrades and completely renovated interior, or heading out for a day to enjoy all Wells has to offer in the summer, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, location, and coastal charm.

The Location

Drakes Island

A quiet, tucked-away neighborhood on one of Wells' most cherished beaches.

Drakes Island sits at the northern end of Wells, Maine — a peninsula framed by a wide sandy beach on one side and the tidal marsh and estuary on the other. It's a place that has held onto its old-fashioned, unhurried charm: no boardwalk crowds, just clean sand, gentle surf, and miles of shoreline to wander.

You're minutes from Wells' lobster shacks and seafood, a short drive to Ogunquit and Kennebunkport, and a stone's throw from the Rachel Carson National Wildlife Refuge for kayaking and birdwatching along the marsh.
